Privacy Policy

Updated on October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information IndiKids Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (the “Company”, “We”, “Us”) collects through the "IndiKids" and "IndiKids Child" apps and website (the “Service”), why we collect it, and the choices you have. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

1. Who this applies to

1.1 Parents of legal age who create an IndiKids account, and children whose device a parent connects using IndiKids Child.

1.2 A child's information is processed only because a parent installed IndiKids Child and gave consent on the child's behalf. By pairing a device, the parent confirms they are the child's parent or lawful guardian.

2. Information we collect

From the parent: your first name, the answers you give during setup (goals and yes/no questions), the rules you set, your subscription status, and the date you accepted these policies.

From the child's device (only after pairing and permission):

  • Child's first name and device name.
  • App usage: which apps were used and for how long each day.
  • Location: the device's GPS location while IndiKids Child is running, and when SOS is pressed.
  • Battery level and whether required permissions are switched on.
  • Websites visited and YouTube searches made through IndiKids Child, including which ones were blocked.
  • Requests for more screen time and any short message the child adds.

We do not collect the content of messages, calls, photos, contacts, passwords, or what is typed in other apps.

3. How we use it

  • To show the parent their child's screen time, location, and browsing and YouTube history.
  • To apply the rules the parent sets: screen time limits, scheduled downtime, blocked apps and websites.
  • To deliver alerts such as SOS and time requests.
  • To operate subscriptions, provide support and keep the Service secure.

We do not sell personal information, show advertising based on children's data, or use children's data for profiling or marketing.

4. Who can see it

4.1 Only the parent account the child's device is paired with can see the child's information. The child can see their own remaining time, rules and requests.

4.2 We use service providers (for example hosting and app-store billing) only to run the Service, under confidentiality obligations. We disclose information to authorities only where the law requires it or to protect a child's safety, as described in section 8 of the Terms of Use.

4.3 Maps are displayed using OpenStreetMap; when the parent opens the map, map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers.

5. How long we keep it

  • App usage, browsing and YouTube history: 30 days.
  • Location: only the latest location is kept.
  • Account information: until the account is closed.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Parents can clear browsing history, remove a child's device, or close the account in the app's Settings. Removing a device or closing the account deletes the related data.
  • You can ask to access, correct or delete your or your child's information by emailing support@indikids.com.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by removing the child's device or closing your account.

We process children's data with verifiable parental consent, consistent with applicable laws including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

7. Security

Devices are linked with single-use pairing codes that expire after 30 minutes, and each device uses its own secret access key. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Changes

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
